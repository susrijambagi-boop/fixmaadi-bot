# FixMaadi Operations: Vendor Onboarding Playbook

**Target Audience:** Bhuvan Nara (Head of Field Operations, Bagalkot) & Onboarding Team.

This playbook outlines how we will build the supply side of our marketplace. In Tier 2/3 cities, service professionals (vendors) are deeply skeptical of "tech companies" taking a cut of their earnings. We must onboard them as **partners, not employees**.

## 1. Scouting & Sourcing

Do not rely on online ads to find professionals. They are found offline.

*   **The Wholesale Hubs**: Visit the wholesale markets (e.g., electrical markets, plumbing supply streets in Old Bagalkot). Plumbers and electricians hang out here in the mornings.
*   **The Competitor's Net**: Speak to security guards at large apartment complexes. They know exactly which local carpenters and maids are reliable and regularly visit the buildings.
*   **The "Respect" Pitch**: When approaching a vendor, do not pitch "an app." Pitch "respect and regular income." 
    *   *Script*: "Anna, we want to give you guaranteed work every week so you don't have to wait for phone calls. You just do the work, we handle the customers."

## 2. Vetting & Verification (The Trust Layer)

Since we cannot build a multi-crore corporate training center like Urban Company, we use **Community Vetting**.

1.  **Reference Checks (Mandatory)**: A vendor must provide 3 phone numbers of previous local customers they have served. Bhuvan's team calls them to verify quality and punctuality.
2.  **Aadhar & Address Verification**: Collect Aadhar cards and physically verify their home address in Bagalkot.
3.  **The "Soft Skills" Filter**: Ensure they don't chew tobacco (gutka) on the job, carry a clean bag, and speak respectfully. 

## 3. Onboarding & Training

> [!WARNING]
> Keep it extremely simple. Tier 2 professionals may not be tech-savvy.

*   **App Training**: Install the "FixMaadi Partner App" on their phone. Show them how to accept a job (Large Green Button) and complete a job.
*   **The FixMaadi Kit**: Provide a branded T-shirt and a standardized ID card. In Tier 2 cities, a uniform commands immense respect and legitimizes their presence in middle-class homes.

## 4. Commission & Earnings Structure

*   **Zero Upfront Fees**: Never charge them to join.
*   **The First 5 Free**: Do not take any commission on their first 5 jobs. Let them taste the money and realize the platform works.
*   **Standard Commission**: After 5 jobs, introduce a flat 10-15% commission (much lower than Urban Company's 20-30%).
*   **Instant Payouts**: Daily settlements via UPI. *This is non-negotiable.* Gig workers in smaller cities need cash flow today, not next week.

## 5. Penalty & Quality Control

*   If a professional is late more than twice, they are paused for 3 days.
*   If a professional is caught bypassing the app (doing a side deal with a customer), they are permanently banned.
*   **The Reward**: Vendors with a 4.8+ rating for the month get a ₹1,000 cash bonus.
