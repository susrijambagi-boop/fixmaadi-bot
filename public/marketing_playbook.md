# FixMaadi: Bagalkot Go-To-Market Playbook

Welcome to the Marketing Department of FixMaadi. As your Virtual CMO, I have designed this playbook specifically for Tier 2/3 dynamics. We will ignore expensive Silicon Valley tactics (like generic Facebook Ads) and focus entirely on hyper-local community integration.

## Phase 1: The "Digital Auto" Strategy (Weeks 1-2)

In towns like Bagalkot, offline visibility drives online trust.

*   **Auto-Rickshaw Branding**: Rent the back panels of 50 local auto-rickshaws operating around key hubs (Navanagar, Vidyagiri, Old Bagalkot). 
    *   *Copy*: "Mixie Kettidya? Puja ideya? FixMaadi!" (Mixie broken? Have a Puja? FixMaadi!)
    *   *Visual*: A QR code directly linking to the app/WhatsApp bot.
*   **Local Hardware & Kirana Store Partnerships**: Distribute FixMaadi stickers to local hardware stores. When someone comes in to buy a spare pipe or electrical wire, the shop owner recommends FixMaadi to install it (we pay the shop owner a small Rs. 20 referral bounty).

## Phase 2: Community WhatsApp Engine (Weeks 3-4)

The primary internet layer in Tier 2 cities is WhatsApp.

*   **Resident Welfare Associations (RWAs)**: Bhuvan must infiltrate local apartment and colony WhatsApp groups. 
*   **The "Forward" Campaign**: Create high-quality, vernacular (Kannada) WhatsApp status videos that are funny and relatable. E.g., A skit of a husband failing to fix a leaking tap, and the wife immediately booking FixMaadi.
*   **WhatsApp Booking**: Integrate a simple WhatsApp Business API so older users don't have to download an app. They can just message "Hi" and book a plumber directly via chat.

## Phase 3: The "Local Influencer" Trust Hack (Weeks 5-6)

*   **Purohit Network**: Purohits are highly respected and enter hundreds of homes. We onboard them for free. When they do a housewarming (Gruhapravesha), they hand out a "FixMaadi Welcome Home" card offering 10% off the family's first deep cleaning or appliance setup.
*   **Micro-Influencers**: Sponsor local Bagalkot meme pages or YouTube vloggers who review local food. Have them do a "Day in the life" where they use a FixMaadi beautician or AC repairman.

## Customer Retention Strategy

> [!TIP]
> **The 10-Minute Call Back**
> In Tier 2 cities, people love the "human touch." After every first booking, our customer success team will personally call the customer to ask, "Did the professional do a good job? Were they polite?" This guarantees a lifetime customer.

## Key Metrics to Track
1.  **Cost of Acquisition (CAC)**: Should be under ₹100 via offline channels.
2.  **Word-of-Mouth (WoM) Coefficient**: Track "How did you hear about us?" on booking. Target 60%+ organic WoM.
3.  **Repeat Booking Rate**: The true measure of trust. Target 40% within 90 days.
