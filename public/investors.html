FixMaadi

A WhatsApp-native, 0% commission home services platform for Bagalkot, Karnataka — no app to download, no commission taken from local workers.

🟢 Live & operational in Bagalkot

What FixMaadi is

FixMaadi connects Bagalkot residents with verified local service providers — plumbers, electricians, beauticians, purohits, carpenters, and more — entirely through WhatsApp. There's no app to install: customers message a WhatsApp number, pick a service from a simple menu, and a local technician is dispatched.

Why it's different

0% commission

Unlike aggregator apps that take 20-30% from workers, FixMaadi charges no commission. A small ₹49 visit fee (adjusted into the final bill if the customer proceeds) funds fair pay for technicians.

No app required

Runs entirely inside WhatsApp, the app every customer already has open. Zero download friction, works on any phone.

Verified providers

Every technician is onboarded with a photo and government ID (Aadhaar) on file before they're allowed to take a booking.

Bilingual by design

Kannada-first, with English available — built for how Bagalkot actually communicates, not translated as an afterthought.

How it works

  1. Customer sends "Hi" to the FixMaadi WhatsApp number.
  2. Bot asks for name, then presents a numbered service menu (works on every phone, no special rendering needed).
  3. Customer confirms address and calling number; a technician is assigned and dispatched.
  4. Customer receives the technician's photo and a one-time verification code before work begins.
  5. After the job, the customer rates the experience directly over WhatsApp.

Frequently asked

Is FixMaadi an app?

No — it runs entirely inside WhatsApp. There's nothing to download.

How does FixMaadi make money without commission?

A small, transparent ₹49 visit fee is charged per booking. If the customer proceeds with the service, that fee is fully credited against the final bill. Technicians keep 100% of their service earnings.

Which city does FixMaadi operate in?

FixMaadi currently operates in Bagalkot, Karnataka, India.

Are the service providers verified?

Yes — every provider is onboarded with a photo and Aadhaar (government ID) on file before taking bookings.
